Limitations

  • Very small sample. 100% and 0% rates are sample facts; month-to-month churn could plausibly be as low as about 59%.
  • Drivers overlap. Contract, tickets and early tenure describe largely the same customers.
  • Association, not cause. The pilot is what tests whether contracts retain customers.
  • No timing, reasons or cost data. Impact is revenue kept, not profit.

Recommended action

  • Offer month-to-month customers a discounted annual plan at 60-90 days.
  • Add a first-year onboarding check-in programme.
  • Trigger proactive outreach on the third support ticket.
  • Run it as a 90-day pilot with a holdout group; success is at least 5 points lower churn than the holdout.
